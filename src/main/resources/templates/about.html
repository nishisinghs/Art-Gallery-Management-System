About Our Art Gallery

Celebrating creativity by connecting talented artists with art lovers through an elegant online platform.

Art Gallery Management System

The Art Gallery Management System is a modern web-based application designed to simplify the management of artworks, artists, exhibitions, and customer interactions. Visitors can easily browse beautiful artwork collections, discover talented artists, and contact the gallery, while administrators can securely manage all gallery content through an intuitive dashboard.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make art more accessible by providing a digital platform where creativity can be shared with everyone. We aim to create a smooth and engaging experience for visitors while helping gallery administrators efficiently organize artworks and exhibitions.

Project Objectives

  • Provide an easy way to browse artworks online.
  • Allow visitors to search artworks by title or artist.
  • Enable category-based artwork filtering.
  • Provide detailed information about every artwork.
  • Allow visitors to contact the gallery directly.
  • Provide administrators with a secure management panel.

Key Features

🎨 Browse Artworks

Explore a collection of paintings with images, artist information, categories and pricing.

🔍 Search & Filter

Quickly locate artworks using keyword search and category filters.

🖼 Artwork Details

View complete information including artist, description, category and price.

📩 Contact Gallery

Send enquiries directly to the gallery using the contact form.

⚙ Admin Dashboard

Securely add, edit and remove artworks through the administration panel.

💬 Message Management

View and manage customer enquiries directly from the admin dashboard.

Technology Stack

  • Backend: Java & Spring Boot
  • Frontend: HTML, CSS & Thymeleaf
  • Database: MySQL
  • Security: Spring Security
  • ORM: Spring Data JPA (Hibernate)

Why Choose Our Gallery?

We combine beautiful artwork presentation with modern web technologies to deliver a fast, secure and user-friendly experience. Whether you're exploring artwork, discovering talented artists or managing gallery content, our platform provides everything in one place.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates the development of a complete full-stack web application using Spring Boot and MySQL. It offers an attractive experience for visitors while providing administrators with powerful management tools for efficiently maintaining an art gallery.
